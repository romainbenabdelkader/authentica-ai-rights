# AUTHENTICA Governance

Status: Draft governance principles for an open application profile.

AUTHENTICA is maintained by AUTHENTICA (France). This document defines
lightweight governance principles for the AUTHENTICA AI Rights & Origin
Manifest while it remains a public draft. It does not create a foundation,
committee, certification body or legal authority.

## 1. Scope and role

AUTHENTICA publishes an open, machine-readable application profile for
declaring content origin, rights reservation, TDM opt-out and AI-training
restrictions. AUTHENTICA provides a verifiable technical artifact only; the
law, audit, the regulator or a court decide legal qualifications.

AUTHENTICA is explicitly NOT a surveillance system, an automated-sanction
mechanism, a DRM or watermarking system, or an automated legal-decision
system.

## 2. Separation from AURA (binding principle)

AURA and AUTHENTICA must remain separate:

- AURA is an open, neutral, non-capturable standard for the technical proof of
  origin and integrity.
- AUTHENTICA is one possible application profile for declaring rights, AI
  restrictions and reservation signals.

AUTHENTICA may use AURA-compatible concepts, but AUTHENTICA does not control
AURA, and AURA does not depend on AUTHENTICA. This separation is a founding
constraint and may not be removed by the maintainer.

## 3. Open draft process

Changes to the profile should be proposed through public, written and
traceable channels:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- documented changelog entries

Private discussions may inform proposals, but normative changes should be
recorded publicly before being accepted.

## 4. Maintainer commitments

While AUTHENTICA remains the maintainer, it commits to:

- keep the profile open and freely implementable under the repository license;
- keep origin and rights signals machine-readable and interoperable;
- never extend the profile into enforcement, surveillance, DRM or watermarking;
- preserve the AURA / AUTHENTICA separation defined in Section 2.

## 5. Evolution

This document is a draft and may evolve before a stable release.
A future multi-stakeholder governance model may be proposed.
